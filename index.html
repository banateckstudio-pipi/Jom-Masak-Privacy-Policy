01 / Overview

About this policy

This policy describes Jom Masak, a cooking and restaurant-management game by Banatek Studios / Banana404 project team ("we", "us"). It covers information used in the game, this website, and privacy inquiries sent to us.

02 / On your device

Data in the game

Jom Masak stores the following information locally on your device so that the game can remember your profile, progress, and preferences:

  • Player profile: the chef name you choose.
  • Game progress: tutorial progress, completed recipe results, scores, stars, completion times, rewards, coins, diamonds, and unlocked ingredients, restaurants, recipes, and minigames.
  • Audio preferences: your music and sound settings.

Your chef name is a local game profile, separate from your Google or Apple account used for purchases. The current game does not provide cloud saves or automatic progress transfer between devices. Please use a nickname and avoid entering sensitive personal information as your chef name.

03 / Accounts & purchases

Store sign-in & in-app purchases

Jom Masak includes in-app purchases. To make an in-app purchase, you must be signed in to a Google account in Google Play on Android, or an Apple Account in the App Store on iOS.

Google Play or Apple's App Store processes your purchase using the account and payment method associated with that store. Enter your account credentials and payment details only through the store's own sign-in and payment screens.

The relevant store handles account, payment, and transaction information under its own terms and privacy policy. See the Google Privacy Policy or Apple Privacy Policy for details.

Signing in to the store does not create a separate Jom Masak online account or enable cloud saving of your game progress.

04 / Collection & sharing

What reaches us

The current game does not send your local profile, progress, or audio preferences to us. It does not integrate advertising or analytics services. We do not sell or share your local game data. Store account and payment processing for in-app purchases are described above.

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your inquiry and resolve the issue you raise. Our email provider processes these messages to deliver and store correspondence. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Google Play, Apple's App Store, your device operating system, and any device backup services you enable may process information separately under their own policies and settings. This policy describes our game and our handling of information.

05 / Your choices

Retention & deletion

Game data stays on your device until it is changed, reset, or removed. You can change your chef name in the game. Reset Progress resets your player profile and game progress; it keeps your audio preferences and is not a complete erasure of every local file.

To remove the app's local data on Android, open your device's Settings → Apps → Jom Masak → Storage → Clear storage / Clear data. Menu names vary by device. This removes your local progress and preferences. Manage any device or cloud backups separately through the relevant backup service.

We cannot restore game progress from our servers because we do not receive or store your local save data. Your local chef profile is not a Banatek Studios online account. Clearing game data does not delete your Google or Apple account or the store's purchase records. Manage those accounts and records through the relevant provider.

We retain privacy and support correspondence only as long as needed to handle your inquiry and meet applicable obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of correspondence; we will explain if any information needs to be retained.

06 / Safeguards

Data security

Game saves and audio settings use local app storage. Save files are not encrypted by the game. Keep your device secure, and avoid including sensitive information in your chef name or support messages. No storage or communication method can guarantee complete security.

07 / Visiting this page

This website

This page does not include advertising, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or contact forms. Contact links open your email application.

When this website is published using GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Pages data-collection information and GitHub Privacy Statement for details. Website hosting is separate from the game's local save data.

08 / Keeping you informed

Policy updates

We may update this policy as the game changes. We will revise the date on this page and provide notice of significant changes through the game or its store listing. Any future data collection, online features, or third-party services will need to be reflected in this policy and the app's disclosures.
